How to Use Fire Safety Equipment

Learn the correct techniques — practice makes perfect

PASS Method – Fire Extinguisher

  1. Pull the pin – This unlocks the operating lever
  2. Aim low – Point the nozzle at the base of the fire
  3. Squeeze the lever – Release the extinguishing agent
  4. Sweep side to side – Cover the entire fire area
Only fight small fires. If fire is large or spreading — evacuate immediately and call 101.

Fire Blanket Usage

  1. Pull the tabs to release the blanket from its pouch
  2. Hold the blanket by the corners, protecting your hands
  3. Gently place/throw it over the flames to smother
  4. Leave it in place for at least 15-20 minutes
  5. Turn off heat source if safe to do so
Ideal for kitchen oil fires, clothing fires & small contained fires.

Smoke & CO Alarms

  • Install on every floor, inside bedrooms & outside sleeping areas
  • Test monthly by pressing the test button
  • Replace batteries yearly (or use 10-year sealed models)
  • Replace entire unit every 8-10 years
  • Never disable or remove batteries

Emergency Escape Plan

  • Know two ways out of every room
  • Practice escape drills twice a year
  • Crawl low under smoke
  • Feel doors before opening (if hot, use alternate exit)
  • Meet at a designated outdoor meeting point
  • Never go back inside for belongings

Important Safety Tips for Indian Homes

  • Keep a fire blanket within arm’s reach of the stove
  • Never use water on oil/ghee fires – it causes explosion
  • ABC dry powder is best all-round for Indian homes
  • Place extinguishers near exits, not inside the kitchen only
  • Check pressure gauge monthly
  • Service extinguishers every year
  • Keep LPG cylinder area clear and ventilated
  • Install smoke alarms even if you have LPG
